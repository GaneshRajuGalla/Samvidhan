Privacy Policy for Samvidhan

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Samvidhan ("the app") is a reference app for the Constitution of India. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is handled.

Data Collection

Samvidhan does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not connect to the internet and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only, using Android's private app storage:

  • Bookmarks — articles and amendments you choose to bookmark
  • Reading history — which articles, schedules, and amendments you have viewed, and when

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us, to Google, or to any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions and no internet access permission.

Third-Party Links

The Settings/About screen may contain links that open in your device's browser or share sheet. Visiting those external pages is subject to the privacy policy of that external site, not this one.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
